About

Benetty is a private, single-user personal assistant application. It is used by exactly one person, the owner of the connected Google account, to read, search, label and draft emails in their own Gmail mailbox. It is not offered to the public and does not accept new users.

Privacy Policy

Data accessed. With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail messages, labels and basic mail settings through the Gmail API.

Use. The data is used only to provide assistant functions to the owner: summarising and searching mail, organising it with labels, and preparing drafts. Emails are sent only after the owner's explicit approval.

Storage and processing. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. To provide the assistant functions, email content may be processed by the AI model provider the owner uses (Anthropic). Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
